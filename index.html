About

Who's writing this

I'm a software engineer at Boeing, working on embedded real-time systems in C++ and C# on VxWorks and RTX64. The interesting part of that job isn't the feature list, it's the constraint: everything has a fixed budget, and correctness includes finishing on time.

That constraint is what I actually enjoy, so I keep chasing it outside work too. Right now that means a C++ inference engine with custom CUDA kernels and an OpenAI-compatible serving layer, plus TracePipe, a tool for replaying and debugging high-throughput Kafka and Redpanda pipelines.

The systems I find most interesting share that DNA: trading infrastructure, ML serving, anything where microseconds and throughput are the product.
